Speak up for animals

The government has invited public comments on the Draft Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (Control and Supervision of Experiment, Breeding and Trading of Laboratory Animals) Rules, 2026.

The draft raises questions about independent oversight of experiments, sourcing of animals, and exceptions involving stray animals. Read the proposal and decide what you want to say.
